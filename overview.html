Overview

A venture built around disciplined claims.

WinVerse™ is defining SerotoniX™ as a development-stage personalised facial-activation wearable for guided wellbeing routines. The public proposition stays deliberately narrower than the underlying technical ambition.

The proposition

State exactly what the product is meant to do.

The first design input is not a circuit or a shell. It is the intended user, use environment and outcome.

“Personalised” refers to fit, placement, configuration and repeatable use. “Guided wellbeing routine” describes an experience objective. Neither phrase proves biological target engagement, clinical benefit or medical-device status.

Three separations

Do not let one kind of evidence impersonate another.

01

Design is not proof

A coherent rendering can establish a product direction. It cannot establish comfort, reliability, safety or benefit.

02

Wellbeing is not a regulatory shortcut

Classification follows intended purpose, claims, design, operation and risk. Marketing language alone does not settle it.

03

Research is not product validation

Published studies can inform hypotheses and methods. Evidence transfers only when the tested device, parameters, population and claim are sufficiently aligned.
