Overview

A consumer category with an evidence gate.

SerotoniX™ is being developed as a personalised, hands-free facial-activation system for premium personal care. The commercial proposition is designed to stand independently of depression, serotonin or medical-treatment claims.

Recommended categoryPersonalised Facial Activation
Initial intended pathwayNon-medical personal care
Current statusDevelopment-stage concept

The proposition

A guided facial ritual—not a therapeutic shortcut.

The intended consumer value is hands-free activation, repeatable positioning and guided session delivery.

“Personalised” should describe controlled adaptations in fit, contact and setup. “Facial activation” describes physical product action. Neither phrase establishes a cosmetic result, sustained wellbeing effect, clinical benefit or regulatory classification.

What creates value

The system around stimulation is the proposed differentiation.

01

Hands-free repeatability

A fitted interface may reduce dependence on manual movement, pressure and technique.

02

Bounded personalisation

Geometry and placement may vary, while safety limits, compatibility and software logic remain controlled.

03

Measurable sessions

Configuration, contact, completion and faults can be recorded and later linked to objective activation measures.

Current boundary

Development-stage personal-care concept. SerotoniX™ is not currently offered for sale and is not represented as diagnosing, treating, curing, mitigating or preventing disease.

Proposed market learning

Paid validation before broad distribution.

The go-to-market strategy proposes a Singapore Founders Programme with approximately 300–500 paid early users, assisted demonstrations and controlled onboarding. The purpose is to measure demand and product reality—not to manufacture social proof.
