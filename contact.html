Collaboration pathways

Where an external partner can create real value.

A useful collaboration has a named decision, a responsible owner, a defined output and an agreed evidence standard. General expressions of interest are less useful than a concrete problem statement.

R

Research and validation

Study design, human factors, feasibility, outcome selection, statistics and independent interpretation.

E

Engineering and productisation

Interface design, electronics, firmware, risk controls, verification, manufacturing and supplier readiness.

M

Market and regulatory strategy

Intended-use discipline, jurisdiction analysis, quality planning, pilots, distribution and adoption economics.

C

Capital and strategic partnership

Funding or partnership tied to a clear development milestone, governance model and use of proceeds.

What to include

Make the first message decision-ready.

A concise note helps the team assess fit without creating unnecessary disclosure or delay.

01

The user or stakeholder problem you are trying to solve.

02

Your current evidence, product stage and unresolved assumption.

03

The contribution, decision or access you are proposing.

04

The expected output, owner, timing and resource commitment.
