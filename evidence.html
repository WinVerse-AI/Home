Evidence

Evidence must follow the exact product and claim.

The relevant question is not whether adjacent technologies have published studies. It is whether the controlled SerotoniX™ configuration supports a defined claim in its intended users and use environment.

Current levelConcept and test planning
Product-specific efficacyNot established
Evidence ruleNo transfer by association

Evidence stack

Different questions require different studies.

Evidence should accumulate in sequence. Clinical work cannot rescue an unstable device, and a successful bench test cannot prove a user benefit.

01

Engineering verification

Output accuracy, limits, fault response, contact behaviour, charging, communications, environmental performance and reliability.

02

Human factors

Can intended users set up, position, operate, stop, clean and recover from errors safely and correctly?

03

Feasibility

Can the intended workflow be delivered, tolerated and adhered to with a controlled product version?

04

Claim validation

Does a suitable study with an appropriate comparator support the exact outcome and population being claimed?

Current ledger

What the evidence supports today.

The current public materials are concept and planning evidence. They should not be read as clinical, regulatory or commercial proof.

Supported at concept level

  • A proposed wearable architecture and interface direction
  • A staged engineering and evidence plan
  • Testable questions for fit, control, usability and risk

Not established

  • Vagus-nerve target engagement
  • Clinical or physiological benefit
  • Regulatory clearance, reimbursement or market adoption

External research

Context is useful. Transfer is dangerous.

Studies of transcutaneous auricular vagus-nerve stimulation can inform study design, sham conditions, parameter reporting and outcome selection.

They do not validate SerotoniX™. Differences in anatomy, electrode location, waveform, dose, comparator, population and endpoint can change interpretation. Product-specific evidence remains necessary.

Wellbeing experience claim

Medical or physiological claim

Controlled product-specific evidence

Audit rule

Every public claim should identify the product version, evidence type, population, comparator, endpoint and remaining uncertainty.
