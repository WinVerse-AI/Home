Development

Resolve product, claim and economic risk in sequence.

The proposed 18-month pathway connects engineering verification, target engagement, human factors, a product-specific consumer claim and a controlled paid launch. No phase advances merely because the calendar moves.

Current gateConsumer intended purpose and fit architecture
Commercial proof pointPaid Founders Programme
Scale conditionBenefit, safety and unit economics

Eighteen-month pathway

Each stage must earn the next.

Dates coordinate work. Exit criteria determine progress. Unresolved classification, fit, use safety, consumer benefit or manufacturing economics should stop the launch sequence.

0–3

Define

Consumer intended purpose and claim ledger

Outputs: target user, personal-care ritual, use environment, net-impression analysis, classification consultations, requirements, preliminary risk file, fit strategy and preliminary BOM. Exit: the proposition can be stated without relying on depression, serotonin or unsupported cosmetic language.

4–6

Verify

Release candidate and core experience

Outputs: controlled hardware and software configuration, output verification, contact monitoring, fitting repeatability, objective facial activation, skin-contact work, formative human factors and support workflow. Exit: intended users can obtain the target session within predefined safety and performance limits.

7–9

Build demand carefully

Demonstrations, partners and qualified waitlist

Outputs: selected studio and hospitality partners, facialist advisory group, closed demonstrations, price testing, refundable-deposit logic and Founders protocol. Exit: qualified interest exists without unrestricted claims or mass inventory.

10–12

Validate

Paid Singapore Founders Programme

Outputs: approximately 300–500 paid early users, controlled onboarding, 8–12 weeks of use, complaints and safety monitoring, fitting and support data, repeat-use behaviour, referrals and a prespecified cosmetic or experience endpoint. Exit: one defensible consumer benefit and acceptable product operations.

13–18

Launch selectively

Singapore DTC and premium partners

Outputs: written classification, stable manufacturing yield, controlled claims, onboarding, inventory, support, recurring-product logic and next-market analysis. Exit: positive contribution economics, acceptable returns and evidence of continued use—not merely initial purchase.

Product gates

The quiet bottlenecks are fit and independent use.

A visually complete mask can still fail as a product. Intended users must position it, understand device state, tolerate the session and recover from faults without continuous expert intervention.

Fit success

Can a practical size or modular-fit system achieve reliable contact across intended users?

Target engagement

Can the final product verify intended facial activation at tolerable settings?

Use safety

Can users recognise readiness, poor contact, faults, completion and contraindications?

Support burden

Do onboarding and troubleshooting remain compatible with consumer-product economics?

Personalisation hierarchy

Test standard sizes first, then modular inserts and app-assisted fit. Reserve fully bespoke moulding for configurations where its incremental value exceeds scan, remake, lead-time and support costs.

Claim-validation gate

The first commercially useful claim must follow the final product.

A beauty-tech launch needs a consumer payoff. Technical activation alone is insufficient.

Candidate endpoints include awake-looking appearance, perceived facial vitality, temporary appearance of lift, facial symmetry during activation, routine satisfaction and preference over a manual routine. The final wording must reflect the actual endpoint, effect, time point and comparator.

Engineering

Verified delivery

Output, contact, timing, configuration and objective activation meet specification.

Human factors

Independent use

Intended users fit, operate, stop, clean and recover safely.

Consumer benefit

Prespecified outcome

The final configuration supports one exact non-medical claim.

Investor-visible milestones

Capital should buy de-risking—not activity.

The supplied strategy proposes a US$4–6 million seed or strategic financing concept. That is an illustrative management proposal, not a current fundraising commitment or valuation.

Product-specific evidence that can survive legal, regulatory and advertising review.

Repeatable unit economics

Manufacturing yield, COGS, channel margin, warranty, returns and contribution margin.

Stop conditions

Do not scale an attractive failure.

The programme should narrow, redesign or stop when a core assumption fails rather than explaining the signal away.
