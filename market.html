The real commercial risk

Category interest can mask weak repeat use.

The decisive signals are paid acquisition, successful onboarding, week-eight retention, product-specific benefit and support economics—not waitlist size or social engagement.

Proceed

Fit and activation are reliable; users report a meaningful benefit; repeat use and unit economics meet thresholds.

Narrow

A specific segment or professional channel works, but mass direct-to-consumer use does not.

Redesign

Comfort, fit, target engagement or onboarding prevent reliable independent use.

Stop

The incremental benefit does not justify price, complexity or regulatory burden.
