What this pillar means

The strategy becomes credible only when the operating requirements are explicit.

This page explains the role of this pillar in the SerotoniX™ thesis and the evidence needed before it can support stronger public claims.

01

Begin with target engagement

Before asking whether users feel or look different, the programme must verify that the intended facial muscles or action units are activated consistently in the final device configuration.

02

Build the claim ladder one level at a time

Engineering delivery, immediate experience, cosmetic appearance, affective outcomes and future regulated applications require different endpoints, study designs and levels of evidence. One broad trial cannot substitute for this sequence.

03

Connect each study to a commercial decision

Research should answer whether to proceed, narrow, redesign or stop. Useful outputs include stimulation-versus-comfort limits, fit reliability, independent onboarding, blinded appearance ratings, repeat use and prespecified affective measures.

04

Treat configuration control as part of scientific validity

Every result must identify the exact shell, contact interface, electrode map, firmware, protocol and fitting process. Evidence generated on a materially different configuration cannot be silently transferred.

05

Qualify institutional relationships accurately

Proposed work with UHN and KITE should remain described as planned and subject to grant approval, final workplans and institutional agreements. Collaboration does not itself validate the product or its claims.

Evidence boundary

More studies are not automatically more evidence.

The expansion engine creates value only when each study tests a defined claim in the intended product configuration, uses appropriate comparators and produces a decision. Exploratory signals, partner prestige and publication count cannot replace target engagement, replication or commercially relevant outcomes.
